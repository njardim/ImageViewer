Qt 6.11.2
=========

imageViewer uses the Qt 6.11.2 libraries and plugins (The Qt Company Ltd. and
other contributors) under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 3
(LGPL-3.0-only). The LGPL-3.0 is a set of additional permissions on top of the
GNU General Public License version 3; both texts are in this folder:
LGPL-3.0.txt and GPL-3.0.txt.

Qt is linked dynamically and ships as separate, unmodified files next to the
application (Windows: the Qt6*.dll files and plugin folders; macOS: the
frameworks in imageViewer.app/Contents/Frameworks and the plugins in
Contents/PlugIns; Linux: lib/ and plugins/). You may replace them with your own
build of a compatible Qt 6.11 release.

Corresponding source code of Qt 6.11.2:
  https://download.qt.io/archive/qt/6.11/6.11.2/  (one archive per module in
  submodules/, e.g. submodules/qtbase-everywhere-src-6.11.2.tar.xz)
  https://github.com/qt/qt5/tree/v6.11.2 (top-level repository; each module,
  for example https://github.com/qt/qtbase, carries the tag v6.11.2)

listed at https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/licenses-used-in-qt.html
